Meet Chef Vic

A life poured
into every plate

Before Haveli, Chef Vic ran hotel kitchens and restaurants across India: feeding banquets, running the pass and learning the regional classics one dish at a time. He brought all of that experience here to Fredericton.

“Haveli is more than just a restaurant — it's my dream come to life.” Chef Vic · Head Chef & Owner

We're proud of the Canadian traditions that made this our home: poutine, pub wings, the Friday fish fry and the long table on a cold night. For Chef Vic, bringing authentic Indian dishes to Canada with a Canadian twist is a dream come true. The makhani goes over cheese curds, the haddock gets an ajwain batter and the striploin goes into the tandoor.

Every plate is meant to delight the senses: the smoke off the tandoor, whole spices hitting hot ghee, and naan torn fresh from the clay oven.
